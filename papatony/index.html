What is Papatony
Papatony is a professional consulting and business support service for foreigners living and working in Korea. From public administration and visa procedures to finance, tax, and labor matters, we provide structured and reliable solutions that help clients settle and grow with confidence.
Why it matters:
Administrative and visa processes remain complex and difficult to access
Growing demand for professional support in finance, tax, and labor issues
Few trusted partners provide tailored, one-stop consulting for foreigners
Core Services
Public service support (administrative simplification, health insurance, certificate issuance)
Professional visa consulting (F-4, E-7 visas, entry/exit procedure guidance)
Comprehensive financial consulting (specialized financial products, career consulting, job placement)
Tax & labor advisory (tax counseling, year-end settlement, worker rights protection)
Track Record
Proven experience handling diverse administrative and visa cases for foreign clients
Executed integrated consulting projects in finance, tax, and labor
Collaborated with institutions and industries across Korea for practical support
REVENUE MODEL
Consulting service contracts
Administrative, visa, and tax representation services
Long-term retainer-based advisory
Vision
We aim to be the trusted partner for foreigners’ settlement and growth in Korea. Papatony connects individuals and businesses across borders, turning experiences in Korea into opportunities for new growth.
