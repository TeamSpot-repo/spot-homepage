CEO / Co-founder
Sophie Chivanova
Sophie is the Co-founder and CEO of SPOT, with a strong background in global strategy and market expansion. She previously launched the European office of MyMusicTaste, expanding operations across 10+ countries. As the founder of Brightside Concerts and NM Music, she built sustainable revenue models and executed joint ventures across Europe and Asia. At SPOT, she leads service development and global partnerships, drawing on her international experience and data-driven approach.
CPO / Co-founder
Asya Guseva
Asya is the Co-founder and Chief Product Officer of SPOT. She has extensive experience in global music business development, having worked on artist partnerships and contract negotiations at Universal Music Group, Sony Music, and Go Music. At SPOT, she oversees product planning and strategic partnerships, bringing deep industry insight and strong execution to the team.
CFO / Corporate Auditor
Min Wonhong
Min is the Co-founder and CFO of SPOT, responsible for legal, finance, and internal operations. He previously managed foreign investment and corporate establishment at Commvault and Clumio, and led a successful ₩6.5 billion fundraising effort at MyMusicTaste. With cross-industry expertise and a focus on operational optimization, he supports SPOT’s growth with a strong foundation in financial and business management.
Software Engineer
Su Kim
Su is a Product Designer and Developer at SPOT, working to build a service that serves as a local friend for foreign tourists. Leveraging her career as a designer, she works on UI design while considering easier user experiences, and as a developer, she works toward the team's vision of helping all foreigners become experts in Korean travel and life through faster product speed improvements and development of various features.
